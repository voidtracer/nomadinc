About Nomad Inc.

I am Stan, a documentary photographer based in Pretoria, South Africa. I used to shoot everything — big weddings, birthday parties, modeling tests — but I found my best work happens when the camera disappears.

Nomad Inc. is built around a single idea: real moments, unposed, captured with a darkroom soul. I shoot with a documentary instinct, emulating the look and grain of film in a darker, moody tone. No direction, no stiff poses. Just what happened.

I no longer shoot large weddings. My physical well-being cannot keep up with 12-hour days on my feet. Instead, I focus on elopements, micro-weddings, intimate events, brand storytelling, and maternity. These are the shoots where I can move quietly and stay present.

If you want photography that feels like a memory rather than a production, get in touch.
