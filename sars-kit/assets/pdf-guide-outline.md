## Part 2: Provisional Tax Deep Dive (Pages 9-18)
- [ ] **What Is Provisional Tax?** — 2 pages
  - Not a separate tax — advance payments on your final liability
  - Two payments: August (1st) + February (2nd)
  - Optional 3rd payment (September) — "top-up"
- [ ] **Calculating Your Payments** — 4 pages
  - Method 1: Prior year safe harbour (easiest, penalty-proof)
  - Method 2: Current year estimate (requires accuracy)
  - Worked example: Photographer + Student (R240k taxable)
  - Worked example: Seasonal income (wedding photographer)
  - Calculator walkthrough (web + Sheets versions)
- [ ] **Safe Harbour Rules** — 2 pages
  - 100% of prior year tax = no penalty
  - 90% of actual tax = no penalty (if no prior year)
  - Underpayment interest (SARS rate + 2%)
  - When to use which method
- [ ] **Filing & Paying** — 2 pages
  - eFiling IRP6 step-by-step
  - Payment methods: EFT, credit card, debit order
  - Reference numbers: PRV + tax year + payment #
  - Proof of payment: save PDF, rename convention
- [ ] **Common Scenarios & Fixes** — 2 pages
  - Income dropped drastically — reduce 2nd payment
  - Forgot to register — voluntary disclosure (VDP)
  - Overpaid — refund process
  - Moved provinces — no change (national)
