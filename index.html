Welcome

I am a documentary photographer based in Pretoria, South Africa. My work is moody, film-inspired, and quiet. I photograph elopements, intimate weddings, private events, brand storytelling, and maternity.

I do not shoot big weddings anymore. My body cannot keep up, and my best work happens in small spaces where people forget the camera is there.

About

Nomad Inc. is built around a single idea: real moments, unposed, captured with a darkroom soul. I shoot with a documentary instinct, emulating the look and grain of film in a darker, moody tone. No direction, no stiff poses. Just what happened.
